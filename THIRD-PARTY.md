## Material Symbols (icons)

Most of `assets/icons/*.svg` are Material Symbols Rounded by Google (filled style, from the
`@material-symbols/svg-400` package), some redrawn or adjusted (paths flattened, viewBoxes moved,
`gauge-balanced.svg` and `skip-triangle.svg` derived from them).
Licensed under the Apache License, Version 2.0: https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0
Source: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons

## Not included

The default font, Google Sans Flex, isn't shipped: install it yourself (SIL Open Font License 1.1,
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Google+Sans+Flex). Without it the island falls back to the system's
sans-serif font. The sounds (`assets/sounds`, made by `tools/make-sounds.py`) and the handwriting
(`assets/handwriting`) are original to this project.
